## Scope and maintenance

The nine scene guides are reading copies of runtime prompts in `app/src/main/resources/owlett/skills/`. Prompts and app behavior are unchanged. AGENTS.md retains the original English workflow with a Chinese companion.

Original legal texts (root LICENSE, chatui/LICENSE, and files under licenses/) and NOTICE attribution are preserved, not replaced with translations. See [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.en.md) for context and scope.

Update both languages together, preserving commands, parameters, versions, data handling, and unverified items. Verify actual published artifacts before replacing the download placeholders in both READMEs. Do not assign invented versions to historical APKs or ZIPs.
